The **Domain Name System (DNS)** is the internet's phonebook. Computers address each other by IP address, but people address websites by name — DNS is what translates `www.example.com` into something like `93.184.216.34`.

## Why it's fast in practice

The full walk above sounds slow, but it almost never happens for real — every step along the way caches its answer for the record's **TTL** (time to live). Most lookups hit a cache at the browser, OS, or recursive-resolver level and never reach a root or TLD server at all.
