Unlike every other widget in this system, `Codec` doesn't simulate anything — it runs the browser's actual encoding functions (`btoa`/`atob` for Base64, `encodeURIComponent`/`decodeURIComponent` for URL encoding) directly on whatever you type. That's why it's the one primitive without a "Simulated" badge.

## Base64

<Codec mode="base64" defaultInput="Hello, nullbase!" />

## URL encoding

<Codec mode="url" defaultInput="a value with spaces & special=characters" />

Try switching either one to "decode" and pasting in the output from "encode" — you should get your original input back exactly.
